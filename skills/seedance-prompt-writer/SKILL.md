name: seedance-prompt-writer
description: The user asks for a video idea, a scene, or a prompt for Seedance video generation.
---

Write the prompt as one paragraph of plain, visual language that a video model can render.

Cover these, in this order:
1. **Subject**: who or what is on screen, with concrete visual details.
2. **Action**: what happens over the clip, in present tense.
3. **Setting**: location, time of day, weather.
4. **Camera**: shot size and movement (e.g. "slow dolly-in", "handheld tracking shot").
5. **Lighting and mood**: light source, color palette, atmosphere.
6. **Style**: e.g. cinematic, anime, documentary, 35mm film.

Rules:
- Describe only what can be seen; skip sounds, smells and inner thoughts.
- Keep it under 120 words.
- After the prompt, add one line starting with "Variation:" that changes the camera or style.
